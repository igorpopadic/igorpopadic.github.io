Our story

Small café, big breakfasts.

We started Sunny Side with one simple idea: breakfast shouldn't end at 11. Everything on our menu is made fresh, with local eggs, sourdough from the bakery next door and beans roasted in small batches.

☕ Specialty coffee Single-origin beans, roasted weekly
🥐 Baked daily Croissants out of the oven at 7:30
🌿 Local produce Eggs, greens and fruit from nearby farms
