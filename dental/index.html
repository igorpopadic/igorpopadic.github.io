FAQ

Questions patients ask us.

Will it hurt?

We use modern local anaesthetic and go at your pace. If you're nervous, tell us — we'll explain every step before we do it, and you can raise your hand to pause any time.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Treatments over €300 can be split into up to 12 monthly payments with 0% interest.

What if I have a toothache right now?

Call us. We keep emergency slots open every day and will try to see you within 24 hours.

Do you treat children?

Yes, from age 3. First visits for kids are short and playful, so they get used to the chair.
