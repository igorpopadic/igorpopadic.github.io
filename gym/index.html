FAQ

Before you come in

Still not sure? Call us — we're happy to help.

Is there a registration fee?

No. You only pay for the plan you choose — no sign-up or card fees.

Can I cancel my membership?

Monthly plans can be cancelled any time before the next payment. 3-month and yearly plans can be paused for up to 30 days (injury, travel, exams).

Is there parking?

Yes — free parking for members right in front of the gym, plus bike racks at the entrance.

What do I need for the student discount?

Just a valid student ID. Show it at the reception when you sign up and the 20% discount applies to every plan.

I've never trained before. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Every new member gets an intro session with a coach who shows you the equipment and builds a simple plan to start with.
