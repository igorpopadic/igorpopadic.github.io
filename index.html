FAQ

Questions clients usually ask.

Something else on your mind? Just ask me.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on how big the site is and what it needs to do. Tell me about your business and I'll send you a clear quote, with no hidden costs.

How long does it take?

A simple landing page can be ready in a matter of days. Bigger sites with more pages or online booking take a bit longer, and I'll give you a timeline before we start.

Do I need to know anything technical?

Not at all. You just send me your information, photos and logo, and I take care of everything else, including putting the site online.

Can I see the design before it's finished?

Yes. You get a preview of the first version so you can tell me what to change before anything is final.

What if I need changes later?

No problem. New prices, photos or opening hours: send me a message and I'll update it for you.
